# IT Helpdesk Guide (sample)

Note: This is sample content for a demo project. It is not a real company procedure.

## VPN connection
To work from home or outside the office and reach company systems, open the VPN app and sign in with your work email. If the VPN does not connect, check your internet connection, restart the VPN app, then restart your laptop. If it still fails, contact IT.

## Password reset and account lockout
Your account is locked after 5 wrong password attempts. It unlocks automatically after 30 minutes. To reset your password, use the self-service password page. IT will never ask for your password.

## Password expiry
Passwords expire every 90 days. You get a reminder 14 days before. Change it from the self-service password page. A new password needs at least 12 characters and cannot repeat your last 5 passwords.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
MFA asks you to approve each sign-in on the authenticator app. If you got a new phone, use the self-service portal to register it before you reset the old one. If you lost your phone, contact IT to remove it from your account. Never approve a sign-in request you did not start; report it to IT.

## Wi-Fi access
Use the "Staff" network with your work email and password. Guests use the "Guest" network; the password is at reception. If the Wi-Fi keeps dropping, turn Wi-Fi off and on, forget the "Staff" network and join it again, and move closer to an access point.

## Email on mobile
Install the company email app from the app store and sign in with your work email. Approve the sign-in request on your phone.

## Outlook not sending or receiving email
Check that Outlook shows "Connected" at the bottom. Close and reopen Outlook, then restart your laptop. Check your Junk folder for missing messages. If emails stay in the Outbox, check the attachment size: the limit is 25 MB.

## Mailbox full
Your mailbox limit is 50 GB. Empty the Deleted Items and Junk folders, and delete old emails with large attachments. Save large files to OneDrive and share a link instead of attaching them.

## Microsoft Teams, camera and microphone
If others cannot hear or see you in a meeting, check that the right camera and microphone are selected in the meeting device settings. Close other apps that use the camera. Unplug and reconnect the headset. If Teams is slow or freezes, sign out, close it fully and open it again.

## Slow computer
Restart your laptop at least once a week. Close browser tabs and apps you are not using. Make sure updates have finished installing. If your disk is almost full, free up space. If it is still slow after a restart, contact IT.

## Blue screen or crash after an update
If your laptop shows a blue screen, write down the stop code shown on the screen and restart the laptop. Most crashes after an update stop after one or two restarts. If it happens again, do not try to fix it yourself: contact IT with the stop code and the time it happened.

## Windows updates
Updates install automatically. Save your work and restart when asked; you can postpone a restart for up to 3 days. Do not turn off the laptop while it shows "Working on updates".

## Disk space full
Empty the Recycle Bin and the Downloads folder. Move large files to OneDrive and set them to "online-only". Uninstall apps you no longer need from the Company Portal.

## Restoring deleted files
Files deleted from OneDrive or a shared folder stay in the Recycle Bin for 30 days. Open the Recycle Bin on the OneDrive or SharePoint site and restore the file. You can also right-click a file and use "Version history" to get an older version. After 30 days, contact IT with the file name, folder and date.

## Shared folder access
To get access to a shared folder, ask the folder owner or submit an access request with the folder name. Your manager must approve it. Access usually works within 1 working day; sign out and in again if it does not appear.

## Software requests
To install new software, submit a software request with the software name and the business reason. Your manager must approve it. Installation takes up to 3 working days. Only install software from the Company Portal.

## Printer problems
If the printer does not print, check that it is on and has paper. Remove the job from the print queue and try again. To scan, use the "Scan to email" button on the printer. If the printer shows an error code, report it to IT with the printer name.

## Monitor and docking station
If the external monitor shows no signal, check both ends of the cable and that the monitor is set to the right input. Unplug the docking station for 10 seconds and plug it back in. Restart the laptop while it is connected to the dock.

## Keyboard, mouse and headset
For wireless devices, replace or charge the batteries and pair the device again in Bluetooth settings. For wired devices, try another USB port. If it still does not work, request a replacement with an equipment request.

## Equipment requests
To request a new laptop, monitor, or keyboard, submit an equipment request. Your manager must approve it. Delivery takes up to 5 working days.

## Lost or stolen device
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to IT immediately, even outside working hours. IT will lock and wipe the device remotely to protect company data. Then change your password.

## USB drives and external storage
Personal USB drives are blocked on company laptops to protect company data. Use OneDrive to share and move files. If your work needs an encrypted USB drive, submit an equipment request.

## Remote desktop
To connect to your office computer from home, connect to the VPN first, then open Remote Desktop and enter your office computer name. Your office computer must be turned on.

## Browser problems
If a website does not load or looks broken, refresh the page, then clear the browser cache and cookies. Try a private window or another browser. Company websites work best in the latest version of Edge or Chrome.

## Phishing and suspicious emails
Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails. Report the email to IT. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, change your password immediately and contact IT.
